Virtual Interview with Praveena AI

Interview Instructions

1. Starting the Interview

  • When you're ready to begin, click the "Start Exam" button.

2. Webcam Setup

  • Ensure you are clearly visible on your webcam throughout the interview.
  • Look directly into the camera while answering questions.

3. Environment

  • Find a quiet location to avoid background noise.

4. Answering Questions

  • After answering each question, click the "Next" button to proceed.

5. Completing the Interview

  • Once you've answered all the questions, click the "Submit" button.
  • Please wait while your interview is being submitted.
